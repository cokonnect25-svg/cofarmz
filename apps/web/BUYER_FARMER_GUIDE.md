# Buyer vs Farmer Profile Guide\n\n## Overview\nFarmRent now supports two types of users: **Farmers** and **Buyers**. Both can use the platform differently, though they share the same interface for browsing and renting machinery.\n\n---\n\n## How to Sign Up\n\n### Farmer Account\n1. Go to **Sign Up** page\n2. Select **\"Farmer\"** button (tractor icon)\n3. Fill in email, password, and name\n4. Complete signup\n5. **Farmer-specific features unlocked:**\n   - Add crops you grow\n   - Set expertise level and experience for each crop\n   - Track expected yield dates and quantities\n   - List your machinery for rent\n   - Searchable by other farmers and buyers\n\n### Buyer Account\n1. Go to **Sign Up** page\n2. Select **\"Buyer\"** button (shopping cart icon)\n3. Fill in email, password, and name\n4. Complete signup\n5. **Buyer-specific features:**\n   - Browse and rent machinery from farmers\n   - Book equipment for your use\n   - Add favorites\n   - Leave reviews after renting\n   - Searchable by farmers (as potential bulk buyers)\n\n---\n\n## Profile Features Comparison\n\n| Feature | Farmer Profile | Buyer Profile |\n|---------|----------------|---------------|\n| **Crops Section** | ✅ Yes - Can add/edit crops | ❌ No (hidden) |\n| **Equipment Section** | ✅ List machinery to rent | ✅ View rented items |\n| **Followers/Following** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |\n| **Leave Reviews** | ✅ After renting equipment | ✅ After renting equipment |\n| **Nearby Search** | 🔍 Searchable by other users | 🔍 Searchable by farmers |\n| **Profile Badge** | 🌾 Farmer Profile | 🛒 Buyer Profile |\n\n---\n\n## Sample Test Accounts\n\n### Demo Farmer Account\n- **Email:** demo@appgen.com\n- **Password:** demo1234\n- **Type:** Farmer\n- **Location:** Agricultural region\n- **Features:** Has crops listed, equipment available\n\n### Sample Buyer Account (Created for Testing)\n- **Email:** buyer@example.com\n- **Name:** Rajesh Patel\n- **Type:** Buyer\n- **Location:** New Delhi, India\n- **Status:** Can search for farmers and equipment\n\n---\n\n## Key Differences in Practice\n\n### When Searching for Users (Nearby Farmers)\n\n#### Farmers see:\n- Other farmers with their crops and equipment\n- Filter by yield date, expertise level\n- Can connect for crop exchange or equipment sharing\n\n#### Buyers see:\n- Farmers with available equipment\n- Other buyers in the area\n- Can view equipment availability\n- Can contact farmers to rent machinery\n\n---\n\n## How to Distinguish Profile Types\n\n### Visual Indicators\n1. **Profile Header Badge:**\n   - 🌾 \"Farmer Profile\" - appears at top\n   - 🛒 \"Buyer Profile\" - appears at top\n\n2. **Expandable Sections:**\n   - Farmers see: Followers, Following, **Crops** (unique), Equipment\n   - Buyers see: Followers, Following, Equipment (no Crops option)\n\n3. **Profile View:**\n   - Both show location, stats (followers, following, equipment count)\n   - Farmers additionally show crops count\n\n---\n\n## Creating a Crop Profile (Farmers Only)\n\n1. Go to **My Profile** → **Crops** section\n2. Click **\"Add Crop\"**\n3. Fill in:\n   - **Crop Name** - Select from 80+ common crops\n   - **Years of Experience** - Your farming experience\n   - **Expertise Level** - Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert\n   - **Expected Yield Date** - When you expect to harvest\n   - **Expected Yield Quantity** - How much you expect to yield\n   - **Unit of Measurement** - kg, tons, quintals, bags, units\n\n4. Click **\"Add Crop\"** to save\n5. Other farmers and buyers can see your crop expertise when they view your profile\n\n---\n\n## Renting Machinery\n\n### Both Farmers and Buyers Can:\n1. Browse available equipment in **Machinery List**\n2. Filter by location, price, rating\n3. View detailed equipment information\n4. Mark equipment as favorite (❤️)\n5. Book equipment for specific dates\n6. Leave reviews and ratings after rental\n\n### Booking Process:\n1. Tap equipment card\n2. View details and owner information\n3. Click **\"Rent Now\"**\n4. Select start and end dates\n5. See total cost calculation\n6. Confirm booking\n7. Owner will accept/decline\n8. Once accepted, you can see owner's phone and message\n\n---\n\n## Search & Discovery\n\n### Farmer Search (Nearby Farmers page)\n\n**Toggle Buttons:**\n- **\"Farmers\"** - See farmers with crops, equipment\n- **\"Buyers\"** - See other buyers in your area\n\n**Filters Available:**\n- **Yield Date Range** - Find farmers by harvest timing (Farmers only)\n- **Location Radius** - Search nearby users\n- **Search Bar** - Search by name or location\n\n**Use Cases:**\n- 🌾 Farmer searching other farmers: Find co-farmers with complementary crops\n- 🛒 Buyer searching farmers: Find equipment owners and negotiate\n- 🌾 Farmer searching buyers: Identify potential bulk customers\n\n---\n\n## Database Structure\n\n### User Table\n```sql\nCOLUMN: is_farmer (BOOLEAN)\n- true  = Farmer account (has crop expertise)\n- false = Buyer account (focuses on equipment rental)\n```\n\n### Farmer Crops Table\n```sql\nid, user_id, crop_name, years_of_experience, \nexpertise_level, expected_yield_date, \nexpected_yield_quantity, expected_yield_quantity_uom\n```\n\n---\n\n## Tips for Users\n\n### For Farmers:\n1. ✅ Add multiple crops to increase visibility\n2. ✅ Set realistic yield dates (buyers may contact for harvest)\n3. ✅ List all available equipment\n4. ✅ Respond quickly to rental requests\n5. ✅ Maintain high ratings for reviews\n\n### For Buyers:\n1. ✅ Browse farmers by location and yield dates\n2. ✅ Check farmer reviews before booking\n3. ✅ Save favorite equipment\n4. ✅ Leave honest reviews after use\n5. ✅ Message farmers for bulk discount negotiations\n\n---\n\n## Future Enhancements\n\nPotential features to consider:\n- Direct buy/sell between farmers and buyers (based on yield dates)\n- Bulk discounts for buyers\n- Equipment swap between farmers\n- Certification badges for farmers\n- Seasonal crop planning calendar\n- Yield forecasting based on historical data\n
